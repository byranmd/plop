# PLOP! 扑通

Fling a big-mouthed frog up a river of lily pads. Over wide water it belly-skips like a stone.

**▶ Play: https://byranmd.github.io/plop/** (desktop or phone browser)

## How to play
- **Drag back from the frog and let go.** The dotted arc shows where you will land.
- **Pull further over wide water** and the frog skips across. Skips add bonus points.
- **Land in the centre of a pad** for a PERFECT and a growing combo. Catch fireflies in mid-air.
- **From 30 m some pads sink.** Jump before the ring drains.
- Four zones as the day fades: Shallows, Reeds, Dusk, Night. How far can you go?

Keyboard: hold Space (or W / ↑) to aim, ← → to turn, release to jump. Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts.

## Made of code
- No image, model, audio or font files. Everything is generated at runtime:
  - river, banks, reeds, lily pads and the frog
  - toon shaders written in TSL (the three.js shading language)
  - the UI, in DOM and inline SVG
  - every sound effect, synthesised with Web Audio, plus seeded lo-fi music
- Seeded rivers: the same seed always builds the same river.
- three.js r187 WebGPU renderer with a WebGL2 fallback. Targets 60 fps, and quality adapts to the device.
- The game rules are pure TypeScript running at a fixed 60 Hz step, covered by unit tests.
- English and 中文.
- About 280 KB gzipped, most of it three.js.
